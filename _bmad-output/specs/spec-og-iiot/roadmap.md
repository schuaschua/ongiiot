# Roadmap After the POC

None of this is in scope for the POC. It frames the next-phase funding conversation and design choices that must not block it.

## Next phase (from the brainstorm's 30-day, ~50-pipe pilot)

- Sensor readiness report across the facility.
- Asset knowledge graph linking pipes, sensors, piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs) and work orders, with tags mapped to assets automatically.
- Outliers among similar pipes across a wider set of pipes.
- Weekly ranked watchlist of pipes at risk.
- A named executive sponsor on the client side.

## Later capabilities, in priority order

1. **Methane and leak detection to MRV grade.** Feeds OGMP 2.0 (Oil and Gas Methane Partnership) reporting and EU MRV equivalence. Target date: before 1 Jan 2027. OGMP 2.0 Level 5 for operated assets is due by Feb 2027.
2. **Inspection business case.**
   - Dynamic risk-based inspection (RBI) scheduling (API 580/581).
   - Evidence packs for extending inspection intervals.
   - A ledger of savings in dollars.
   - Engaging the regulator in year 1.
3. **Commissioning baseline.** Learn each pipe's normal behaviour over 90 days and remove daily and seasonal ambient cycles.
4. **Starting data for the models.** There is no failure history yet. Fill the gap by digitising paper inspection records, running rule-capture workshops with veteran engineers, and flagging deviations from physics-based hydraulic models.
5. **LNG-specific analytics.** Cryogenic cooldown rate, insulation cold spots, hydrate risk, corrosion under insulation, and remaining life.
6. **Fleet learning.** Scan the whole fleet for signatures that preceded past failures, and spread risk from inspection findings to similar pipes.

## Trust ladder

The client moves through these stages at its own pace: observe → advise → automatic work orders (SAP PM or Maximo) → automatic control. The POC covers the observe stage and the start of the advise stage.
